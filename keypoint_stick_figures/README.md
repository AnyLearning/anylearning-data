# Generated Keypoint Stick Figures

- **Task:** Keypoint Detection
- **Licence:** CC0 1.0
- **Splits:** 24 training images, 8 validation images
- **Schema:** `head`, `left_hand`, `right_hand`, `left_foot`, `right_foot`

Every 512 × 384 image contains two coloured stick-figure instances. Each
instance has all five landmarks; a small deterministic subset marks the right
hand as occluded to exercise COCO visibility without removing its coordinate.

Upload `train.zip` and `valid.zip` to the matching subsets of an AnyLearning
**Keypoint Detection** project. The archives are standard COCO keypoint data
and create the ordered landmark labels automatically.

The pixels and annotations are generated by
`examples/keypoint_detection/generate_dataset.py` in the main AnyLearning
repository. They contain no third-party material and are dedicated to the
public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

This dataset demonstrates import, grouped instances, visibility, training and
inference. Its regular geometry is deliberately not representative of a
real-world accuracy benchmark.
